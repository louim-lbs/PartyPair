The official app takes a dozen steps to rebuild the stereo link between two JBL PartyBox speakers, and that link does not survive switching them off. Party Pair does it in a single tap.

One button wakes both speakers, rebuilds the stereo link and connects the audio. A second tap puts them back to sleep, fading the sound out first.

It can also do it on its own:

* Wake-up: the speakers switch on ahead of your alarm and start your playlist
* Sleep timer: delayed shutdown with a countdown in your notifications
* Bass, volume, balance between the speakers, left and right channels
* Widget, quick-settings tile and shortcuts for routines and Home Assistant

The speakers must be paired in stereo once using the official JBL app. From then on, Party Pair rebuilds the link by itself.

The proprietary BLE protocol used by PartyBox speakers was documented nowhere. It was reconstructed by cross-referencing Bluetooth captures with a decompilation of the official app, and is fully written up in the repository.

Verified on two JBL PartyBox 710. The protocol should hold across the range, but nothing else has been tested.

An independent project, unaffiliated with Harman or JBL.
